## Content Production Workflow

1. **Ideation** — Pull from backlog or respond to current events/trending topics
2. **Draft** — Write in `marketing/drafts/` directory
3. **Review** — Self-edit, check for AI slop (see `feedback_no_ai_slop.md`)
4. **Publish** — Post to channel, move to `marketing/published/`
5. **Distribute** — Cross-post, share on other channels
6. **Measure** — Track engagement 48h after posting, note what worked
