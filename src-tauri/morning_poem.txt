Morning's Gentle Awakening

Golden fingers stretch across the sky,
Painting clouds with amber light,
As darkness slowly says goodbye
To welcome dawn's emerging sight.

The world awakens from its dreams,
Birds begin their morning song,
While dewdrops catch the early beams
And pull the sleepy day along.

Coffee steams in ceramic cups,
The air is crisp and clean and new,
As nature gently wakes things up
With morning's soft and gentle hue.

A fresh beginning starts today,
With possibilities untold,
As morning chases night away
And stories wait to unfold.Dawn's Gentle Whisper

The world awakens with a gentle sigh,
As golden threads pierce through the darkened sky.
Dewdrops dance on petals, fresh and bright,
While shadows flee before the growing light.

The robin's song cuts through the morning air,
A melody that lifts away despair.
Coffee's aroma mingles with the breeze,
As sunlight filters softly through the trees.

Each breath of dawn brings promise, hope renewed,
A canvas blank where dreams can be pursued.
The morning calls with whispers sweet and low,
"Rise up, dear heart, and let your spirit grow."

In these quiet moments, peace unfolds,
A precious gift more precious than pure gold.
For in the morning's gentle, warming embrace,
We find the strength to meet each day with grace.
